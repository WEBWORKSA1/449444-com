FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why is the number 4 unlucky in Chinese culture?

Four (四 sì) sounds almost the same as death (死 sǐ) in Mandarin and Cantonese. Many buildings skip the 4th floor, and buyers often avoid 4 in phone numbers, plates and addresses. One study found Vancouver houses with numbers ending in 4 sold about 2.2% cheaper in Chinese-majority areas.

What does 449444 mean?

Read literally in Chinese it is sì sì jiǔ sì sì sì, dominated by the avoided digit 4. Modern readings reframe it: 94 = 就是 (exactly), 44 = 事事 (every matter), 9 = 久 (lasting). In numerology its digits add to 29, which reduces to master number 11. It also contains 444, the most-searched angel number.

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8 (八 bā), because it sounds like 发 fā, to prosper. 6 (溜, smooth) and 9 (久, long-lasting) are also highly favoured. Combos like 168 (一路发, prosperity all the way) and 520 (我爱你, I love you) are popular in prices and dates.

Are angel numbers and Chinese lucky numbers the same thing?

No. Chinese number luck comes from how the digits sound. Angel numbers are a Western spiritual idea based on repeating sequences. Numerology reduces digits to a root. We show all three side by side.
