About us

Every number has a story.

449444 is an independent platform that decodes numbers across cultures, so people and businesses can choose them with confidence and with a smile.

Why “449444”?

Read in Chinese, 4-4-9-4-4-4 is 四四九四四四. Most listeners hear the sound of 死 (death) five times. That would make it one of the least “lucky” six-digit numbers you could pick. Yet in Western numerology it adds up to 29, then to 11, a master number of illumination. It contains 444, the angel number of protection. And to a young Chinese internet user, 94 means 就是: “exactly”.

One number, many meanings. That is our thesis, and it is why we built one place to see them all side by side.

Our mission

  • Make cross-cultural number meaning free, fast and accurate.
  • Help people avoid costly cultural mistakes in prices, addresses, launches and gifts.
  • Celebrate number culture with humour and respect, without fear-mongering.

Methodology & editorial standards

  • Chinese luck score (0–100): each digit carries a weight based on its widely documented homophones (8 = 发 highest, 4 = 死 lowest). Known combinations (for example 168, 520 and 250) add or subtract points. The score reflects traditional perception, not prediction.
  • Angel-number signal: measures repetition, mirroring and sequences, the features Western angel-number traditions emphasise.
  • Numerology: Pythagorean digit reduction, keeping the master numbers 11, 22 and 33.
  • Sources: peer-reviewed economics research, reputable cultural references and native-speaker review. We cite sources on pillar pages and correct errors quickly.
  • Independence: sponsored content is always labelled. Advertisers never influence scores.
